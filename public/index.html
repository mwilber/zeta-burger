FLIGHT MANUAL

Welcome to the night shift.

Start at the gas station on pad 6. Choose Casual (4 orders), Normal (6), or Expert (8) before starting. Orders arrive at random intervals at pad 0, even while you are delivering. Land there for 0.8 seconds to load the oldest waiting order. Carry one package at a time and deliver it to the highlighted pad.

  • Arrow keys / W A D: thrust up, left, and right. Release to coast.
  • G / Space: extend or retract gear. Sideways thrust only works with gear retracted. Gear does not stop your momentum. The first upward thrust after landing automatically retracts it.
  • Land gently: both feet must fit on the pad, vertical speed under 85 and sideways speed under 35. Only your landing gear can touch a pad.
  • Watch the tip: every order starts at $5–$10 when it arrives. Waiting and onboard tips fall independently: $0.06/sec on Casual, $0.08/sec on Normal, $0.12/sec on Expert. Negative tips subtract from your bank.
  • Fuel: upward and sideways thrust burn fuel; coasting is free. Land at pad 6 to refill at 25% per second for free. With an empty tank, you can only coast and lower your gear.
  • Level ends: deliver all orders, or the shift ends once every order has arrived and every remaining tip is below zero.
  • Three saucers: terrain, buildings, pad sides, and world edges destroy your ship. An order survives a crash, but its tip keeps dropping.
  • P / Escape: pause. On touch screens, hold the arrow buttons and tap GEAR. Turn your phone sideways for a bigger view.
